01 — Methodology

How the counts are made

Hurst's principles of nominality and synchronicity, executed with Bressert's timing bands. CMT-informed, disclosed in full.

Every count in this letter descends from a single assumption: that price is the sum of a small number of nominal periodicities whose troughs synchronise, and that those periodicities are stable enough to be dated in advance and falsified in public.

Every count descends from one assumption: that price is the sum of a small number of nominal periodicities whose troughs synchronise, and that those periodicities can be dated in advance and falsified in public.

01 / NOMINAL MODEL

Hurst's nominal set, unmodified

The 18-year, 54-month, 18-month, 40-week, 20-week, 40-day and 20-day periodicities are used as published. No curve-fitted lengths, no proprietary "discovered" cycles. Where an instrument does not respect a nominal band, it is reported as not respecting it.

Published periodicities used as published. No curve-fitted lengths, no proprietary "discovered" cycles.

02 / TIMING BANDS

Bressert timing, stated as a window

Troughs are published as dated windows with a stated tolerance, never as a single day. A window that closes without a trough is recorded as a miss and carried forward in the accuracy log rather than quietly re-dated.

Troughs are published as dated windows with a tolerance, never a single day. A window that closes empty is recorded as a miss.

03 / TRANSLATION

Translation before conclusion

Right or left translation of the prior completed cycle is measured before any statement about the next one. Translation is the only directional inference the letter draws from cycle structure; everything else is timing.

The only directional inference drawn from cycle structure. Everything else in the letter is timing.

04 / FALSIFICATION

Each count carries its invalidation

No count is published without the price or date that would break it. When a count breaks, the following issue opens with the break rather than burying it. The letter contains no signals, no entries, and no exits.

No count is published without the price or date that would break it. Breaks open the next issue.

02 — Coverage

Seven instruments, counted every week

Coverage is deliberately narrow. Each instrument has a documented nominal set and a count maintained without gaps.

SPX S&P 500 Index Primary nominal set. Cleanest 20-week structure of the seven. 40D · 20W · 40W · 18M
NDX Nasdaq 100 Index Tracks SPX at the 40-week; diverges materially at the 40-day. 40D · 20W · 40W
GC Gold, continuous Strong 18-month periodicity; 20-week count carried with tolerance. 20W · 40W · 18M · 54M
SI Silver, continuous Synchronises with gold at the 18-month, not reliably below it. 20W · 40W · 18M
DXY US Dollar Index Used chiefly as a cross-check on the metals counts. 20W · 40W · 18M
CL WTI Crude Oil Event-prone. Counts published with the widest tolerance of the seven. 40D · 20W · 40W
TNX US 10-Year Yield Counted on yield, not price. The 54-month structure dominates. 40W · 18M · 54M
Instruments are added only when the data supports a documented nominal set. None are planned. Coverage is deliberately narrow. None planned.
