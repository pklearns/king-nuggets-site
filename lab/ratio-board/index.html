Legend

Cloud W / Cloud M — n/5 ↗

Ichimoku 5-box score on weekly and monthly bars: Tenkan above Kijun, close above Kijun, close above the close 26 bars back, Span A above Span B, close above the cloud. ↗ marks a rising cloud. Scores are for the numerator: 0/5 on A/B is 5/5 for B/A.

Stack W — BULL · BEAR · mixed

Weekly moving averages 20/50/75/110/200/250/300 (200 is an EMA). BULL: fully ordered, all rising, close above every average. BEAR: the mirror image. Anything else is mixed.

200-wk / 300-wk

Close above or below the 200- and 300-week simple average, and whether that average is rising or falling over four weeks. n/a means not enough history.

Unsupported by design: the chart is followed manually in TradingView because its data is not available from the scorer's source; it is not an error. — no data: the symbol could not be scored this week and is shown as unavailable, never estimated. Status is carried by glyph and text, never by colour. Scores and states only; no prices are published.
