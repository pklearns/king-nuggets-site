Legend

Cloud W / Cloud M — n/5 ↗

Ichimoku 5-box score on weekly and monthly bars: Tenkan above Kijun, close above Kijun, close above the close 25 bars earlier (the 26th bar counting the current one), Span A above Span B, close above the cloud. ↗ marks a rising cloud. Scores are for the numerator: 0/5 on A/B is 5/5 for B/A.

Stack W — BULL · BEAR · mixed

Weekly moving averages 20/50/75/110/200/250/300 (200 is an EMA). BULL: fully ordered, all rising, close above every average. BEAR: the mirror image. Anything else is mixed.

200-wk / 300-wk

Close above or below the 200- and 300-week simple average, and whether that average is rising or falling over four weeks. n/a means not enough history.

Regime panel

Risk-off pressure: how many of BIL/SPYG, SHY/SPYG, IEF/SPYG, XLP/XLK, SPLV/SPY and AGG/SPY score 3/5 or more on the weekly cloud; an unscored ratio is shown as UNAVAILABLE and the count is out of the scored ones. Growth leadership: how many scored Growth Leadership ratios are at weekly 5/5. Breadth, credit and rates repeat the board's own scores for RSP/SPY, IWM/SPY, IJR/SPY; JNK, BKLN, LQD (with the 200-week state); US10Y and IEF. Alignment counts how many scored rows fall in each alignment state.

Changes vs last week

Every symbol is re-scored from the same data cut at the prior Friday close, then compared with this week. Listed: weekly or monthly scores moving into or out of 5/5 or 0/5, weekly stack changes, 200-week above/below flips, and Medium or Long letter-grade changes (Short grades are shown on the board but not listed as changes). The monthly comparison uses the monthly bar as it stood at each Friday.

Short · Medium · Long — grades v1 (2026-09-26)

Each horizon adds cloud (0–5) + stack (0–2) + trend (0–2) = 0–9 points, shown as points × 100 ÷ 9 rounded to a whole number, and a letter: A 80+, B 60–79, C 40–59, D 20–39, F under 20. Short: daily cloud; 8/20/50-day stack (BULL 2, mixed 1, BEAR 0, same rule as the weekly stack); close above the 50-day average +1; 50-day average higher than 5 days earlier +1; daily bars end at the Friday close. Medium: weekly cloud; weekly stack (BULL 2, mixed 1, BEAR 0); close above the 50-week average +1; 50-week average higher than 4 weeks earlier +1. Long: monthly cloud; above the 200-week average +1, 200-week rising +1; above the 300-week average +1, 300-week rising +1. n/a: not enough history for a part, never estimated. Rows in each section are sorted by Long, then Medium, then name.

Alignment

Checked in this order, first match wins: Aligned up — all three grades 60 or more; Aligned down — all three 40 or less; Pullback in uptrend — Long 60+ and Short 40 or less; Rally in downtrend — Long 40 or less and Short 60+; otherwise Mixed. n/a if any horizon is n/a.

Colour

One six-step scale, dark to light: 0/5 red, 1/5 orange, 2/5 burnt orange, 3/5 grey, 4/5 teal, 5/5 green. Letters: F red, D orange, C grey, B teal, A green. Stack: BEAR red, mixed grey, BULL green. Lightness rises with each step so the order survives greyscale and colour-blind viewing; the text in every cell carries the value on its own.

Display names

Exchange prefixes are dropped (XLK/SPY, SPX, US10Y); futures and spot gold use plain names (Copper/Gold, Crude/Gold, Crude, Gold/SPX). Hover a row for the full TradingView symbol; the scores JSON keeps it. Each section opens with a one-line reading key: what a high score means there.

Method note

Ratio universe and Ichimoku 5-box scoring adapted from techniques Chris Ciovacco (Ciovacco Capital) has demonstrated publicly. Independent implementation, not affiliated.

Unsupported by design: the chart is followed manually in TradingView because its data is not available from the scorer's source; it is not an error. — no data: the symbol could not be scored this week and is shown as unavailable, never estimated. Colour repeats the text in each cell and is never the only signal. Scores, states and grades only; no prices are published.
