Action Implied

The independent call remains correction, but Tuesday materially strengthens the stabilizing qualifier.

The strongest evidence is not gold itself. It is the combination of silver resilience, 3.6%--4.4% gains across all four miner ETFs, and junior-miner outperformance while gold bullion fell. If that leadership persists and gold subsequently turns higher, the evidence would begin to resemble an early trend repair rather than a temporary bounce.

Do not upgrade on miner strength alone. A bull-trend call requires gold to stop making lower settlements, regain meaningful ground toward the $4,563/oz August month-end reference, retain silver leadership, and ideally receive help from a weaker dollar and less restrictive real-rate environment.

For staged de-risking on a future advance, do not mechanically wait for the January high. Begin reducing if gold approaches major recovery references while miners stop confirming, ETF flows fail to reaccelerate, futures positioning becomes materially crowded, or silver transitions from leadership into vertical speculative acceleration.
