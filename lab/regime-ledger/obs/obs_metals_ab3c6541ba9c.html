Action Implied

Maintain Correction with a Stabilizing direction.

The improvement is real but incomplete. Gold and silver stopped extending the liquidation even as the dollar strengthened and the 10-year Treasury yield reached its highest level since 2002. Meanwhile, October Fed-hike odds fell materially. That combination argues against describing the regime as deteriorating.

It does not yet justify accumulation or bull-trend. A stronger tactical setup requires verified easing in real yields, a sustained weaker dollar, continued gold support above the recent lows, stronger silver relative performance, and broad same-session confirmation from GDX, GDXJ, SIL, and SILJ.

The secular metals case remains materially stronger than the tactical setup because reserve diversification, central-bank demand, Chinese physical demand, and near-record ETF holdings continue to absorb unusually severe rate pressure.
