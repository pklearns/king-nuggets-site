The negative evidence is repeated Nasdaq new-low dominance, semiconductor downside momentum, lagging small caps, a rising-dollar/oil combination and elevated pre-holiday borrowing-cost context. Bond-holiday conditions reduce cross-market confidence; the cross-market component was scored from eligible October 12 volatility, liquid ETF and currency/commodity observations, not prior cash-bond levels.

Educational Summary

A Neutral regime with an intact large-cap primary trend and deteriorating speculative leadership often describes a market that is selectively resilient rather than broadly confirmed. Broad one-day advances can coexist with a weakening longer-horizon internal structure. The distinction between durable participation repair and a defensive rotation requires repeated improvement in highs/lows, moving-average breadth and small-cap relative strength, alongside cross-market confirmation from the next live cash-bond session. This is an educational description, not an action recommendation.
