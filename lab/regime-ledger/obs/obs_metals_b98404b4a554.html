Tactical and Secular Probabilities

Tuesday was constructive: gold and silver rose together while the dollar and Treasury yields eased. That is the first combination in several sessions that looks like genuine repair rather than simple resilience. It is not enough to end the correction because long yields remain exceptionally high, December tightening is still heavily priced, and broad miner confirmation cannot be scored.

Secular 12–24 Months: Bull 58% / Base 28% / Bear 14%.

The secular case remains stronger. Persistent ETF participation, central-bank reserve diversification, fiscal-financing stress, and silver's structural deficit continue to support precious metals even under an unusually restrictive rate regime.
