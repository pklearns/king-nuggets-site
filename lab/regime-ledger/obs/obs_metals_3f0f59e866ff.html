What Changed

Gold rebounded from the prior session despite another rise in real yields. Reuters reported spot gold at $4,414.30/oz, December futures settling at $4,458.80/oz, and silver at $67.91/oz.

Source: Reuters, 2026-09-09.

The U.S. Treasury's September 9 real-yield curve showed the 5-year at 2.20%, 7-year at 2.32%, 10-year at 2.46%, 20-year at 2.79%, and 30-year at 2.98%. The 10-year real yield rose three basis points from September 8.

Source: U.S. Department of the Treasury, 2026-09-09.

Gold investment demand strengthened materially in the latest monthly data. The World Gold Council reported $18 billion of global gold-ETF inflows in August, the second-largest monthly inflow on record, with holdings rising 121 tonnes to a record 4,189 tonnes.

Source: World Gold Council, 2026-09-09.

Treasury-financing stress remained visible. Reuters reported Treasury would conduct a September 10 buyback of up to $6 billion in 10- to 20-year bonds, while the benchmark 10-year nominal yield reached 4.8528% after the announcement.

Source: Reuters, 2026-09-09.

Macro / Flow / Positioning Evidence

Monetary policy and real yields

The 10-year real yield at 2.46% is a direct September 9 Treasury observation and remains a material headwind to non-yielding bullion. Reuters reported markets pricing roughly a 60% probability of a 25 bp Fed hike at the September meeting as investors awaited PPI and CPI.

Sources: U.S. Department of the Treasury, 2026-09-09; Reuters, 2026-09-09.

U.S. dollar

Reuters described the U.S. dollar as hovering near a two-week low during the September 9 gold rally. The softer dollar provides support, but it has not yet overwhelmed the real-yield headwind.

Source: Reuters, 2026-09-09.

ETF and fund flows

The World Gold Council's August ETF report showed $18 billion of global inflows, with global holdings rising to a record 4,189 tonnes. North America added $7.7 billion, Europe $7.9 billion, Asia $2.0 billion, and other regions $234 million.

Source: World Gold Council, 2026-09-09.

The latest LSEG Lipper weekly data reported by Reuters, covering the week through September 2, also showed gold and precious-metals funds continuing to attract capital for an eighth consecutive week. This broad precious-metals signal supports silver modestly, but the gold-specific ETF evidence is substantially stronger.

Source: Reuters/LSEG Lipper, 2026-09-04.

Central-bank and reserve demand

China added roughly 20 tonnes of gold to official reserves in August, extending its reported buying streak. The latest broader World Gold Council data also showed continued official-sector accumulation.

Sources: People's Bank of China data reported September 2026; World Gold Council, September 2026.

CFTC positioning

The latest official CFTC report available at report time is positions as of September 1, released September 4. Gold speculative positioning remained elevated but had eased from the prior week; silver positioning was materially less crowded. Because CFTC data are published weekly, this is the current available observation rather than a carried-forward daily score.

Source: CFTC Commitments of Traders, positions as of 2026-09-01.

Physical supply and demand

The World Gold Council's latest quarterly supply-and-demand report showed Q2 total gold demand including OTC at 1,269 tonnes, bar-and-coin demand of 307 tonnes, and second-quarter mine production of 966 tonnes.

Source: World Gold Council Gold Demand Trends Q2 2026, 2026-07-30.

The Silver Institute's current 2026 annual outlook expects a sixth consecutive silver-market deficit and forecasts the deficit at 46.3 million ounces, with global mine production broadly flat.

Source: Silver Institute World Silver Survey 2026, April 2026.

Fiscal and Treasury financing

Reuters reported Treasury's September 10 buyback operation would purchase up to $6 billion of 10- to 20-year securities. Treasury had already signaled larger long-end liquidity-support buybacks. The effect remains two-sided: persistent fiscal and market-function concerns support gold structurally, while higher term premium and real yields remain a tactical headwind.

Source: Reuters, 2026-09-09; U.S. Department of the Treasury, August 2026.

Action Implied

The evidence supports bull-trend without an upgrade to bull-acceleration.

Gold's structural case strengthened through exceptional ETF inflows and continued official-sector demand. Silver's September 9 outperformance and structural deficit are constructive. Against that, the 10-year real yield rose to 2.46%, Fed-hike odds remain material, and the required miner-confirmation component is unavailable.

A softer inflation path plus verified miner leadership would materially strengthen the acceleration case. Sticky inflation, still-higher real yields, and failure of gold to hold the rebound would move the evidence toward correction.

The later cutoff helped with Treasury data but did not fully solve miner-close publication lag. If full miner confirmation is operationally important for every daily run, the evidence from this session argues for moving both regime reports later than 8:30 p.m. ET rather than weakening the v0.3 availability rule.
