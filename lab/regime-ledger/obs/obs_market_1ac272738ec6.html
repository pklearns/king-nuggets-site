Educational Summary

A Defensive reading with an index-level relief rally typically describes a market where short-covering or concentrated leadership can lift benchmarks without repairing the underlying participation or intermediate trend. The more durable regime change would require a cluster of broader advancing participation, fewer new lows, improving small-cap/equal-weight relative strength, sustainable sector leadership, and non-deteriorating credit/rate confirmation. These are diagnostic conditions, not personalized positioning instructions.
