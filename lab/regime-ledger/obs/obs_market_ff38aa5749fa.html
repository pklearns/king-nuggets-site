Educational Summary

Wednesday moved the market away from an immediate Defensive downgrade, but not back to Risk-On.

The two-session sequence now reads:

Tuesday: indexes ↓ + 2.8-to-1 negative breadth + lows expand + Russell ↓ + oil/rates ↑ + VIX ↑
Wednesday: indexes ↑ + ~1.8-to-1 positive breadth + Russell leads + equal weight confirms + VIX ↓ — but new lows remain elevated and oil/rates remain restrictive.

That pattern is consistent with a stabilization attempt inside an intermediate correction, not yet a completed regime repair. The rebound becomes more persuasive if positive breadth persists while new lows contract and 50-day participation moves back above 50%. Conversely, another failure of breadth accompanied by expanding lows would restore the Defensive transition signal quickly.
