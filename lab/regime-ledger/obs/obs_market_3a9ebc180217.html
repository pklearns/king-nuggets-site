Current conclusion

NEUTRAL — Moderate-High confidence — Mixed / failed follow-through, but not renewed deterioration

The market remains in a transition state. A stronger Risk-On reading would normally require multiple sessions of positive breadth, renewed Russell/equal-weight confirmation, improving Nasdaq high/low participation, and stable-to-lower long yields. A move toward Defensive would require expanding new lows, broader sector weakness, materially higher volatility, and clearer credit deterioration.

The highest-information observation from Wednesday is therefore not the S&P's −0.02%. It is that Tuesday's breadth repair did not persist, but the deterioration still has not spread far enough across price, volatility and long-term participation to establish a defensive regime.
