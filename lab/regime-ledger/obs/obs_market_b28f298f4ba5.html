Contradictions: health care/staples/energy were firmly positive, equal weight held up better than cap weight, and the official long Treasury yield declined after the CPI shock. Educational Summary

A Defensive regime means the distribution of observed equity risk has worsened: broad benchmarks and higher-beta groups weaken together, former leaders lose momentum, and defensive industries outperform. It is a description of the completed market environment, not a forecast that losses must continue and not a personalized action recommendation. The distinction between a defensive equity tape and a full credit/liquidity crisis matters: credit-spread and broad exchange internals would need independent confirmation before drawing the stronger conclusion.
