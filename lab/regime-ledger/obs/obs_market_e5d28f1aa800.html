AI hardware / semiconductors retained selective strength. Intel rose 9% and Qualcomm 3.2% after a custom-AI-chip agreement with Amazon (Reuters, 2026-09-08). That leadership is meaningful but narrow.

Software remained a clear laggard. The S&P 500 software and services index fell 1.4%, its second consecutive decline (Reuters, 2026-09-08), as investors reassessed AI disruption risk.

Small caps did not confirm a broad risk-on rotation. Russell 2000 declined 0.5% while the Nasdaq fell only 0.32% (Associated Press and Reuters, 2026-09-08).

Interpretation: Durable leadership exists, but it is concentrated in energy and selected AI infrastructure rather than a broad pro-cyclical advance.

Tuesday weakens the stabilization case established on September 2–4.

The useful sequence is:

September 1: breadth and macro conditions deteriorated sharply.
September 2–3: breadth, volatility and rates improved together.
September 4: stronger payrolls re-tightened rates, but internals held up better than the indexes.
September 8: breadth weakened materially, equal weight lagged, oil rose, long yields exceeded 4.8%, and new lows expanded again.

That is enough to move the evidence meaningfully toward the defensive side of Neutral, but not enough to change the regime itself.

The distinction is horizon-dependent:

Primary trend: positive.
Intermediate trend: weakening.

Conversely, a renewed breadth recovery with lower oil and yields would support the interpretation that the market remains in an intermediate correction within an intact primary uptrend.
