Action Implied

The independent call remains correction, with direction still stabilizing rather than weakening.

Monday is a modest setback to the repair thesis. Gold and silver gave back part of last week's rebound even though oil and long nominal yields fell. That says the stronger dollar and expected Fed path remain binding constraints.

At the same time, Monday did not erase last week's evidence. Gold remains above its post-Fed low, silver remains comparatively resilient, and real yields eased from Friday. The evidence therefore supports correction-with-stabilization rather than renewed acceleration lower.

A bull-trend upgrade requires gold to reclaim and sustain progress toward the $4,563/oz August month-end, silver to resume leadership, real yields and the dollar to ease, and all four miner ETFs to confirm. A break below Monday's $4,322.19/oz gold low accompanied by renewed dollar/real-yield strength would instead shift the direction back toward weakening.

For staged de-risking on a future advance, do not require old highs. If gold approaches the August reference while miners lag, ETF flows fail to reaccelerate, futures crowding rises materially, or silver becomes vertically extended, begin staged reductions before waiting mechanically for the prior peak.
