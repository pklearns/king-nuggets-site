What Changed

The clearest change was a broad macro shock against metals.

Reuters reported August PPI up 0.4% month over month and said Fed-hike odds rose to roughly 70%, from about 62% previously. Spot gold fell to $4,355.85/oz and silver to $64.19/oz. December gold futures settled at $4,407.30/oz.

Source: Reuters, 2026-09-10.

Oil intensified the inflation problem. Reuters reported Brent settling at $107.63/bbl and WTI at $102.48/bbl after another escalation in tanker and shipping disruptions.

Source: Reuters, 2026-09-10.

Nominal Treasury yields also surged. Barron's reported the 10-year Treasury yield reaching 4.927%, while the Wall Street Journal reported a 4.943% 10-year yield late in the session. Because those are nominal yields rather than the required official real-yield observation, they are discussed but do not substitute into the unavailable real-yield component.

Sources: Barron's, 2026-09-10; Wall Street Journal, 2026-09-10.

Macro / Flow / Positioning Evidence

Monetary policy and real yields

Scored status: UNAVAILABLE.

Reuters reported September Fed-hike odds rising to about 70% after stronger-than-expected PPI. That is current and relevant. However, the September 10 Treasury real-yield curve was not retrievable at report time, so the combined 25-point component is formally UNAVAILABLE under v0.3.

Source: Reuters, 2026-09-10.

U.S. dollar

The dollar strengthened on the session. The Wall Street Journal reported its Dollar Index at 95.18, up 0.35%. Reuters also described a stronger U.S. dollar as a factor weighing on gold. This is scored as a negative currency impulse for metals.

Sources: Wall Street Journal, 2026-09-10; Reuters, 2026-09-10.

ETF and fund flows

The World Gold Council's August report remains the latest monthly global gold-ETF dataset. It showed $18 billion of inflows, the second-largest monthly inflow on record, with holdings rising 121 tonnes to a record 4,189 tonnes.

Source: World Gold Council, 2026-09-09.

This remains one of the strongest bullish structural inputs in the framework and is not negated by a single down session.

Central-bank and reserve demand

World Gold Council data showed central banks bought a net 23 tonnes in July, with China adding 20 tonnes and Poland 8 tonnes.

Source: World Gold Council, 2026-09-03.

China then reported another 650,000 ounces, about 20.2 tonnes, added to reserves in August, extending its reported buying streak to 22 months.

Source: People's Bank of China / SAFE data reported 2026-09-08.

CFTC positioning

The latest official CFTC release available at report time is still the report updated September 4, covering positions as of September 1. That weekly dataset showed gold speculative length still elevated but reduced from the prior week, while silver remained materially less crowded.

Because the CFTC release schedule is weekly, this is the latest available observation and is eligible for this component.

Source: CFTC Commitments of Traders, updated 2026-09-04.

Physical supply and demand

Gold physical fundamentals remain mixed rather than tight enough to offset the macro shock. The World Gold Council's latest quarterly report showed strong investment and official demand but also healthy mine supply.

Source: World Gold Council Gold Demand Trends Q2 2026, 2026-07-30.

The Silver Institute expects a sixth consecutive annual silver-market deficit in 2026, forecasting a 46.3 million ounce shortfall while mine supply remains broadly flat. That structural deficit remains a meaningful positive input even as price weakened sharply on September 10.

Source: Silver Institute World Silver Survey 2026, 2026-04-15.

Fiscal and Treasury financing

Treasury's enlarged long-end buyback program did not stop yields from rising. Reuters reported that the September 10 buyback was set at up to $6 billion in 10- to 20-year bonds, but longer-term yields remained elevated amid concern over debt supply and inflation.

Source: Reuters, 2026-09-10.

The fiscal impulse remains two-sided: persistent debt and market-function concerns are structurally supportive for gold, while the associated term-premium and yield pressure are tactically adverse.

Alternative Opportunity

No clearly superior liquid-market opportunity is identified.

Source: Reuters, 2026-09-10.

Short-duration Treasuries remain a defensive alternative, but they do not replicate metals' longer-run exposure to reserve diversification, fiscal stress, and official-sector demand.

Action Implied

The evidence now supports correction, not bull-trend stabilization.

That call is driven by a synchronized adverse macro move: stronger PPI, higher Fed-hike odds, surging oil, rising nominal Treasury yields, a stronger dollar, a greater-than-1% gold decline, and a 4.6% silver decline. The inability to retrieve same-day real yields and all four miner closes lowers confidence but does not justify carrying prior bullish component scores forward.

The secular case remains intact. Record gold-ETF holdings, continued central-bank purchases, fiscal stress, and the silver deficit argue against interpreting this correction as a secular regime failure.

The next decisive inputs are the September 11 CPI release, the September 11 CFTC update, and the September 15–16 FOMC meeting.
