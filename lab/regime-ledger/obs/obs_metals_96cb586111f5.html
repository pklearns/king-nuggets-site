Macro / Flow / Positioning Evidence

Policy and Treasury financing. The September 17 Fed hike remains in force; the Fed stopped aggregate securities runoff in December 2025 and now rolls over Treasury principal and reinvests agency principal into Treasury bills. That liquidity-maintenance state is less adverse than continuing QT, but does not negate higher policy rates. October 20 futures favor an October pause and further tightening later. Fiscal 2026's US$1.99tn deficit (6.2% of GDP) and US$1.36tn of gross debt-service outlays underscore Treasury-financing pressure. This is long-run supportive of reserve-diversification demand but near-run potentially bearish through term premia and real yields. Sources: Federal Reserve, September 16, 2026; Reuters citing U.S. Treasury, October 16, 2026; CDES, October 20, 2026.

Gold flows and reserve purchases. The World Gold Council's September ETF inflows (US$10bn) lifted holdings to 4,256t, despite falling gold prices. August reported official buying was 39t; China's September reserve additions of roughly 23t indicate ongoing sovereign demand. These are separate datasets/periods and must not be summed as if comparable. The WGC's Q2 central-bank demand estimate was 289t, while Q2 gold jewellery demand fell 17% y/y; the physical picture is therefore constructive but uneven. Sources: World Gold Council, July 30, October 6 and October 7, 2026; China SAFE/PBoC September reserve report, October 2026.

Silver flows, physical market, positioning. Rolling-month SLV creations are positive, while SIVR shows redemptions; a selective ETF sample cannot establish global silver-fund demand. The April World Silver Survey forecasts a 46.3Moz 2026 deficit (versus 40.3Moz in 2025), with 2026 industrial use expected to contract approximately 3% as photovoltaic thrifting outweighs some electrification growth. This is a forecast, not observed daily scarcity. Managed-money silver net longs at 5,619 contracts are unusually light; positioning has de-risked without producing miner leadership. Sources: Trendonify, October 20, 2026; Silver Institute/Metals Focus, April 15, 2026; CFTC/XOOMAR, October 13/16, 2026.

Catalysts: October 27–28 FOMC, forthcoming PCE inflation, volatile Gulf oil/shipping developments, U.S. housing weakness, Treasury term-premium sensitivity, and possible stress in debt-funded AI investment. Sources: Federal Reserve, September 2026 calendar; Reuters, October 20, 2026; Financial Times, October 20, 2026.
