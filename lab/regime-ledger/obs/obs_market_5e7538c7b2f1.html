Leadership and Rotation

Growth leadership strengthened. Communication Services gained 3.56% and Information Technology 2.76% in WMTMT's closing sector dataset. Consumer Discretionary gained 1.06%. Source: WMTMT, 2026-09-21.

Semiconductors were the dominant industry group. Reuters reported the PHLX Semiconductor Index up 4.3%, while AMD rose 9.6% to $613.31 and reached a $1 trillion market capitalization. Source: Reuters, 2026-09-21.

This is increasingly durable leadership rather than a one-day anomaly: semiconductors also led late last week. The weakness is breadth outside capitalization-weighted growth, not the quality of the technology leadership itself.

Risks and Contradictions

Evidence supporting Risk-On: record Nasdaq; S&P near its record; positive S&P, Nasdaq-100, Dow and broad-universe participation; strong semiconductor leadership; growth-sector leadership; falling Treasury yields; and sharply lower oil.

Evidence limiting confidence: RSP rose only about 0.28% versus 1.49% for the S&P; Russell gained only 0.52%; three sectors declined; Fed policy remains restrictive; and verified same-session new-high/new-low, moving-average breadth, volatility-close and credit-close inputs were incomplete.

The central contradiction is:

The market has broad positive participation, but the magnitude of the advance remains heavily concentrated in large-cap growth.

That is sufficient for Risk-On, but not for high confidence.

Current conclusion

RISK-ON — Moderate-High confidence — Improving

The most important change is confirmation. Technology leadership is no longer operating against uniformly weak participation and rising macro pressure. Breadth turned positive, the Nasdaq reached a record, the S&P approached its record, Treasury yields eased and oil fell sharply.
