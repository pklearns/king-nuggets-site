  1. The two-day rebound failed: Gold and silver fell on October 29, and all four miners declined after rising October 27–28. The breadth of miner selling matters more than the previous session's isolated stabilization.
  2. The macro catalyst became concrete: BEA's October 29 advance estimate reported third-quarter real GDP growth of 3.4% annualized, versus 2.2% in Q2; September PCE inflation increased 0.5% month over month, and Reuters reported 3.6% year over year.   4. The GSR moved further into gold outperformance: Thursday's synchronized spot GSR reached 77.10. That is a daily/week-to-date snapshot, not a second weekly close above 75 and therefore not an invalidation confirmation.
