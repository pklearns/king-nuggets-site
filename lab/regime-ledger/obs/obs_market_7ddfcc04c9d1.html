Risks and Contradictions

Constructive evidence: S&P ended its losing streak; Russell and equal weight outperformed; advancers beat decliners; Treasury yields reversed lower; AI-linked earnings continue to support technology.

Defensive evidence: week-to-date index returns remain negative; new lows remain extreme; HYG made a new 52-week low; VIX rose slightly despite higher equities; the dollar strengthened; and long Treasury yields remain at historically restrictive levels even after Thursday's reversal.

Thursday produced a real one-day improvement in participation and rates, but credit and new-low breadth did not confirm enough repair to end the Defensive regime.
