Tactical and Secular Probabilities

Tactical 1--6 Months: Bull 18% / Base 40% / Bear 42%.

The tactical distribution improved modestly because October hike odds fell sharply and the dollar eased from Tuesday's two-month high. It remains unfavorable because the 10-year real yield reached 2.93%, the nominal 10-year reached 5.29%, gold finished September down sharply, and verified broad miner confirmation is unavailable.

Secular 12--24 Months: Bull 52% / Base 31% / Bear 17%.

The secular case remains moderately positive. Gold ETF demand and central-bank accumulation remain supportive, fiscal and Treasury-financing stress persists, and silver retains a structural supply-deficit backdrop. Sustained real yields near 3%, however, materially raise the opportunity cost of non-yielding metals.

What Changed

The most important improvement was in expected Fed policy. Cooler August PCE inflation pushed the market toward an October pause: CME FedWatch showed 37.1% odds of a hike and 62.9% odds of no change. Source: CME FedWatch as reported by Barron's and Investing.com, 2026-09-30.

The long end moved the other way. The Treasury's September 30 curve showed the 10-year nominal yield at 5.29%, up 3 basis points on the day, and the 30-year at 5.64%, up 5 basis points. The 10-year TIPS real yield was 2.93%. Source: U.S. Treasury data as reproduced by Arcane Intel, 2026-09-30.

Gold failed to capitalize fully on the softer policy path. COMEX gold settled at $4,155.60/oz and ended September down 6.22%. Source: Wall Street Journal, 2026-09-30.

Rates Expectations: CME FedWatch showed 37.1% odds of an October 25-basis-point hike and 62.9% odds of no change after cooler inflation data. Source: CME FedWatch as reported by Barron's and Investing.com, 2026-09-30.

Dollar: Reuters reported DXY at 101.47 during the session, while Investing.com's daily historical series recorded 101.34. Sources: Reuters and Investing.com, 2026-09-30.

Fiscal supply, elevated debt-service costs, and term-premium pressure remain secular arguments for monetary hedges, but their immediate transmission remains adverse through high real yields.

Major Macro Catalyst: August PCE inflation was softer than expected, reducing immediate Fed-hike odds, while elevated energy prices and persistent fiscal concerns kept long-term Treasury yields under pressure. Sources: Reuters, Barron's, and Wall Street Journal, 2026-09-30.

Alternative Opportunity

Intermediate-duration U.S. Treasuries have become more interesting, but no clearly superior asymmetric directional opportunity is identified.

A 10-year Treasury yield around 5.3% provides materially more carry and valuation cushion than earlier in the cycle, and the sharp reduction in near-term hike odds improves the setup. However, fiscal supply, term-premium pressure, elevated energy prices, and the new multi-decade yield highs argue against calling duration clearly superior yet. Short-duration Treasuries and cash remain the cleaner tactical parking place.

Action Implied

Maintain Correction, but improve direction from Deteriorating to Stabilizing.

The September 30 evidence is mixed rather than uniformly bearish. Cooler PCE inflation materially reduced the probability of an October hike and the dollar eased, but real yields remain exceptionally restrictive and the long end of the Treasury curve reached new multi-decade highs. Gold did not produce a convincing breakout from the correction, and complete same-session miner confirmation could not be verified.

Do not treat softer Fed expectations alone as an accumulation signal. A stronger tactical setup would require the improvement in policy expectations to transmit into lower real yields, a sustained weaker dollar, stronger silver relative performance, and verified broad confirmation across GDX, GDXJ, SIL, and SILJ.

The secular metals case remains intact but is not strong enough to override the current tactical correction.
