Educational Summary

A neutral, improving market combines a constructive bounce and better participation with still-unrepaired intermediate price structure. Historically, positive stock breadth and falling volatility can precede more durable advances, but a single rebound after sharp technology losses does not establish a renewed broad uptrend. The evidence is stronger than Friday's defensive reading because today's components were independently rescored, not because the prior label was mechanically adjusted; continuing confirmation would require broader leadership, sustained participation and improved index trend alignment.
