Stage definitions

Concept

The question exists. No scope, no method.

Specced

Purpose, boundaries, and validation approach are written down. No indicators selected, nothing built.

Researching

Inputs are being selected and justified against the spec.

Testing

Implementation exists and is being run against data.

Validating

Results are being checked against the pre-registered question, including for a null result.

Candidate

Validated. Under evaluation for production use.

Graduated

Moved to the primary King Nuggets research product.

Failed

A project that fails validation is recorded as failed, not removed.

Definitions are quoted from LAB_STATUS.md. Stage is carried by the label and the grouping. It is never encoded in colour.
