§ 03 — Article ■ Running [DATE] Sample — illustrative

Sample article title — illustrative

Standfirst slot. Two sentences at most, sentence case, stating what was built and what the reader can verify. It does not preview a conclusion the body cannot support.

Body copy runs at this measure throughout, sentence case, one column. The paragraphs on this page describe the slot they occupy rather than carry example findings, so that no number on the page can be mistaken for a result.

Where the finished article would quote a figure, this template prints an em dash. Where it would name a date, it prints a bracketed token.

01Section heading, sentence case

Sections are numbered in mono and set in the serif. The serif never sets a number; the number is always the mono element beside it.

02Inline evidence

A claim in the prose can carry its evidence directly beneath it. The callout holds command output verbatim, unedited, and is marked as evidence rather than as illustration.

Pull quote slot. Serif, no quotation marks, no attribution unless the source is named in the body.
